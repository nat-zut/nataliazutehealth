Questions

Good to know

Do you work online?

Yes. All sessions are by video, so you can join from anywhere in the UK or Europe.

Who do you work with?

Adults aged 18 and over. Clients with long-term health conditions are very welcome too, and I work alongside your GP or specialist. Nutritional therapy and coaching don't replace medical care.

For some needs, a specialist is the right first step, so I'll help you find the right support:

  • an eating disorder, confirmed or suspected
  • cancer, if you're having treatment or still taking cancer medication
  • serious mental health concerns that need specialist care
  • symptoms a doctor should check first, such as unexplained weight loss or bleeding

Not sure? Ask me on a free call.

Do I need tests?

Tests are optional, but they're often really helpful. They can give us a clearer picture of what may be behind how you feel, so your plan is even more personal to you. If a test would help, I'll explain why and tell you the lab's own price before you decide, with nothing added by me. Reading your results with you is included in your follow-ups.

Do I need supplements?

Supplements are optional, and food always comes first in your plan. Most plans include a few supplements for a limited time, as support alongside the changes we make. I'll explain why each one is there, how long to take it and what it costs. You get 10% off supplements ordered through me, and you're always free to buy them elsewhere.

What if I need to move a session?

I prepare for every session in advance, so I'd really appreciate at least 48 hours' notice if you need to move or cancel. Life happens, though. If something comes up at short notice, let me know as soon as you can and we'll find a way together.
