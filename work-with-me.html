What to expect

Tests and supplements

Tests

Most plans include at least one test. A test can give useful clues about what may be behind how you feel, so your plan can be more personal. Every test is different, so I'll explain why I'm suggesting it and give you one all-in price before you decide, covering the test kit, delivery and the lab analysis. The choice is always yours. We go through your results together, and that's included in your session: on the programme, your week-6 review; otherwise, a nutrition follow-up session (£155 or €185).

Supplements

Most plans include a few supplements for a limited time, chosen for your needs. Food and daily habits still come first. As my client, you get 10% off supplements. I'll explain why I'm suggesting each one and tell you the cost before you decide, and the choice is always yours.

Before you book

Questions

  • How is this different from seeing my GP?

    I don't diagnose or treat medical conditions. I look at food, stress, sleep and daily habits, and help you make changes that fit your life. If you have a medical condition, I work alongside your GP, not instead of them.

  • Will I get a strict diet or meal plan?

    Most of the time, no. Most plans don't need a strict diet, and there are no daily meal plans. We build a few realistic changes around the food you like and the week you have. Sometimes a more structured way of eating helps for a short time. If so, I'll explain why and for how long, and it's your choice.

  • Do I need tests?

    Not always, but most plans include at least one. A test can give useful clues about what may be behind how you feel. I'll explain why and give you the all-in price before you decide, and it's always your choice.

  • Do I need supplements?

    Most plans include a few, for a limited time and chosen for your needs. Food and daily habits come first. You get 10% off, I'll tell you the cost before you decide, and it's always your choice.

  • What if I have a hard week?

    That's normal. We look at what happened and pick it up again from there, without guilt.

  • Is it all online?

    Yes. All sessions are on video, so you can join from home.

  • Who do you work with?

    Women who want to understand their body and are ready to make a few changes. Adult women of every age are welcome (18 and over), including those with a medical condition, alongside their GP.

  • Can I pay in instalments?

    Yes. The programme can be paid in 3 monthly payments that add up to the same price as paying in full.

  • Can I change my mind?

    Yes. You have 14 days after booking to cancel. If you ask me to start within those 14 days and then cancel, you pay only for the part of the service you've already had. Full details are in my Terms.
