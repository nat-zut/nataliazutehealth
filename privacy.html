Privacy policy

This page explains what information I hold about you, why I hold it, and what you can ask me to do with it. Health information is treated with particular care.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Natalja Zute, trading as Natalia Zute Health. I am the data controller. You can reach me at hello@nataliazutehealth.com.

I am registered with the Information Commissioner’s Office as a data controller.

2. What I collect, and when

  • When you book a call: your name, your email address and anything you choose to write in the message box.
  • When you become a client: your health questionnaire (including your date of birth, your health history and your GP’s details), your plan, any test results you share with me, and my notes from our sessions.
  • Only if you agree: recordings of our sessions, and transcripts and draft notes made with an AI tool.
  • When you pay: the amount, the date and a reference. Card details go straight to the payment provider; I never see or hold them.
  • When you ask for the free diary: your first name, your email address and the focus you choose.
  • When you send a message through the contact form: your name, your email address and your message.
  • When you use the website: cookies and similar technology, as set out in the Cookie notice.

3. Why I use it, and my lawful basis

  • To arrange and deliver your sessions — because it is necessary for our contract.
  • Health information in your questionnaire, plan and notes — this is special category data. My lawful basis is your explicit consent, which the questionnaire asks for separately. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Recording our sessions, and using an AI tool for transcripts and draft notes — only with your explicit consent, which I ask for on a separate form with its own yes and no boxes. Saying no won’t change your sessions or your price, and you can change your mind at any time.
  • To keep records I am required to keep, for tax and for my professional insurance — a legal obligation and my legitimate interest in defending a claim.
  • To send you the free diary — because you asked for it.
  • To send you my emails — only if you ticked the box. You can unsubscribe from any email, and I will stop.

I do not sell your information, and I do not use it to make automated decisions about you.

4. Who else sees it

Only the services I need to run the practice, and only what they need:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts this website, and Google (Google Workspace), which runs the booking calendar, email and video calls
  • Stripe, which takes payments
  • MailerLite, which I use to send the diary and my emails
  • the secure form tool for the health questionnaire [name the tool once chosen]
  • the client portal where your plans, documents and messages are kept [name the platform once chosen]
  • the AI tool I use to transcribe sessions and draft notes, only if you agree [name the tool once chosen]
  • my accountant, for invoices and tax records
  • your GP or another health professional, only if you ask me to or agree to it
  • a supplement supplier, only if you ask me to order something for you
  • my professional insurer and legal advisers, only if they are needed to deal with a claim
  • BANT or the CNHC, only if a complaint is made about my work
  • a laboratory, if you choose to have a test, and only with your agreement

With your agreement, I may discuss your case with a mentor or supervisor to keep my practice safe and up to date, with nothing that identifies you. I only share your feedback or a testimonial with your written permission.

If I believe your life or someone else’s is in serious danger, I may share the information needed with the emergency services or your GP, even without your consent.

Some of these services may store information outside the UK. Where they do, they must protect it to UK standards.

Recordings and AI tools

If you agree, I may record our coaching and nutrition sessions. I use recordings to improve my practice and, for coaching, for my International Coaching Federation (ICF) accreditation. A recording may be heard by my mentor coach or an ICF assessor, and only first names are used in it.

If you agree, an AI tool turns our conversation into text and helps me draft my notes. I always check and correct the notes myself, and the AI doesn’t make decisions about you. I only use a tool that signs a UK data protection agreement with me and doesn’t use your information to train its AI.

Both are optional and separate. If you say no to either, I simply take notes by hand.

5. How long I keep it

Enquiries that do not become bookings: up to 12 months. Client records, including your questionnaire, plan and session notes: [check the period your insurer and BANT require; commonly 7 years, or until a child turns 25]. Invoices and tax records: 6 years plus the current year. Email list: until you unsubscribe.

Session recordings: deleted once they have been used for my training or accreditation, and within [12 months] at most, or sooner if you ask. AI transcripts: deleted once I have written up my notes, within [30 days].

6. Your rights

You can ask me for a copy of what I hold, ask me to correct it, ask me to delete it, ask me to limit how I use it, object to my using it, or ask for it in a portable form. Where I rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

Write to hello@nataliazutehealth.com and I will reply within one month. There is no charge.

Some information I have to keep even if you ask me to delete it, for example invoices and the records my insurer requires. I will tell you if that applies.

7. Keeping it safe

Your records are kept in password-protected accounts with two-factor logins turned on, and are not shared with anyone outside the list above. Paper notes, if any, are kept locked.

If something goes wrong and your information is at risk, I will tell you and the ICO where the rules require it.

8. Complaints

Please tell me first. If you are still unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk, or by calling 0303 123 1113.
