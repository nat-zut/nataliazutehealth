My story

Doing it imperfectly beats not doing it at all.

Gut problems run in my family, but for a long time I didn't think about it. Then in 2010 I moved to the UK, and everything changed at once: a new country, culture shock, university and a full-time job. That's when my own digestion problems began.

My gut was all over the place. I was foggy and tired, my skin flared up and I caught every bug going. For years I tried to ignore it. Tablets helped while I took them, and then it all came back. At the same time I was vegetarian, then vegan, for about ten years, and my diet wasn't balanced for me. My energy kept dropping, I was losing weight, and my digestion and hormones got worse.

At some point I knew there had to be another way, so I trained as a nutritional therapist. It brought some uncomfortable truths: the way I lived didn't suit my body or my family history. What helped was not another rule. It was finally understanding how food, stress and sleep work together in my own body, and changing things one step at a time. Later I trained as a wellness coach, because knowing what to do is only half of it. The other half is making it work in a real, busy week.

I'm not perfect, and I'm still on this path. Recent viruses and stress set me back, and I'm finding my way again. I just do what I can, every day.

That's why I'll never ask you to be perfect. The women I work with often tell me a story like mine: tired, bloated, overwhelmed by advice and quietly blaming themselves. Together we look at the whole picture and find steps that fit your life. With me, you won't be judged, and you won't have to work it out alone.

How I work

What I believe

  • You are resourceful and whole.

    I believe every woman is naturally resourceful and whole, not a problem to be solved. As your coach, I'm an ally: I support you and ask the questions, and the decisions stay yours.

  • You know your life best.

    I bring the nutrition knowledge; you know what will work in your week. We write the plan together.

  • Understanding comes first.

    I explain why each change matters, so you can make good choices on your own.

  • Small steps that last.

    One change you keep is worth more than ten you drop.

  • No judgement, no guilt.

    A hard week is part of it. We pick it up again from there.

  • Alongside your GP.

    If you have a medical condition, I work alongside your GP's care, not instead of it.
