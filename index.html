How I work

A plan we create together

  1. 1

    Understand.

    You fill in a questionnaire first, so every session is prepared. We look at what may be behind how you feel, and I explain why each change matters.

  2. 2

    Plan together.

    We write your plan around your real week. If something won't work for you, we change it.

  3. 3

    Make it stick.

    Coaching sessions help you keep going. When a week goes wrong, we pick it up from there, without guilt.

No restrictive diets. No daily meal plans. No guilt.

About me

Meet Natalia

My family has a history of gut problems, and in my 20s they started for me too. I know what it's like to try one approach after another and still not feel like yourself. That's why I trained as a nutritional therapist, and then as a wellness coach. Knowing what to do is only half of it. The other half is making it work in a real week.
