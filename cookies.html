1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. Some are needed to make the site work. Others watch how the site is used, or help with advertising.

2. What this site uses

  • Essential cookies. These keep the site working, remember your cookie choice and keep the booking and payment steps secure. They cannot be switched off, and they do not need your consent.
  • Analytics cookies. These count visits and show which pages people read, so I can make the site better. They are only set if you accept them. [name the analytics tool once chosen]
  • Advertising cookies. None at the moment. If I ever use them, this page will say so and you will be asked first.

3. Your choice

The banner that appears on your first visit lets you accept or decline the cookies that are not essential. Declining is one click, the same as accepting.

You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer, or by clearing cookies in your browser.

4. Embedded content

Some pages may show content from another service, such as the Cal.com booking calendar or a video. That service may set its own cookies and has its own privacy notice.
