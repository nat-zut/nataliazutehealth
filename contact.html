Let's talk

The easiest way to start is a free 20-minute call on video. Choose a time that suits you below. You can also book a Health Review straight away.

Choose what to book

Free fit call

A relaxed chat about what's going on and whether we're a good fit. I don't give advice on this call, because good advice needs your questionnaire and the full picture. There's no obligation to book.

Health Review

Your first full consultation: 90 minutes on video. After booking, I'll send your health questionnaire to fill in beforehand. You'll receive a written plan, with a recommended test if useful, and we'll go through it together in a 30-minute session about 3 days later.

£225 or €265. Not sure yet? Start with the free call.

Booking

How booking works

  1. 1

    Pick a time for your free call and add your name and email. If you like, add one sentence about what you'd like help with, but please no detailed health information; we'll talk about it on the call.

  2. 2

    You'll get an email confirming the time, with a video link.

  3. 3

    After the call, if we're a good fit, I'll send you a link to book your Health Review or programme. There's no obligation to book.

When you book a Health Review or the programme

You choose your option and how you'd like to pay: in full, or in 3 monthly payments for the programme. You'll see the total price before you pay.

After booking, I'll send your health questionnaire. Because it asks about your health, it asks for your clear consent to use that information for your care. I keep it secure and confidential.

At booking you'll see two tick boxes

I agree to the Terms and the Privacy policy.
I'd like my sessions to start within the next 14 days. I understand that if I cancel during that time, I'll pay for the part of the service I've already received.

The second box is optional: without it, your first session is after the 14 days.

Moving a session

Need to move a session? Let me know at least 48 hours before, and we'll find a new time. Sessions cancelled with less notice count as used, unless there's an emergency.
